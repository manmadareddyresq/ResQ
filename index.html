ResQ

Every Second Matters

ResQ is building a real-time emergency transport network that connects patients with the nearest available ambulance in seconds. Our mission is to reduce emergency response time using a smart digital platform that links ambulance providers with people who need urgent medical transportation.

The Problem

During emergencies, finding an ambulance quickly is still difficult in many cities. Patients often face delays due to fragmented systems, manual phone calls, and lack of real-time availability information.

Long Response Times

Ambulances often arrive late due to inefficient dispatch systems.

No Real-Time Visibility

Patients cannot see which ambulance is closest.

Manual Coordination

Most ambulance bookings happen through phone calls.

Fragmented Networks

Independent ambulance providers are not digitally connected.

The ResQ Solution

ResQ connects patients and ambulance providers through a smart platform that instantly identifies the nearest available ambulance and dispatches it to the patient’s location.

Real-time ambulance discovery

GPS location tracking

Smart dispatch system

Live ride tracking

How It Works

1. Request

User opens the app and requests an ambulance.

2. Match

The platform finds the nearest available ambulance.

3. Dispatch

The driver accepts the request instantly.

4. Track

User tracks ambulance arrival in real time.

Platform Model

ResQ operates as a digital platform connecting independent ambulance operators to patients who need emergency transportation. Instead of owning vehicles, the platform builds a strong network of existing ambulance providers.

Business Model

ResQ operates on a commission-based platform model. Ambulance providers receive ride requests through the driver app and earn from each completed trip. The platform takes a small commission for enabling the connection.
