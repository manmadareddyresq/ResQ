The Problem

Medical emergencies require immediate response, but ambulance access is still fragmented and inefficient.

In many cities, patients often face:

Long ambulance wait times
Difficulty finding available ambulances nearby
Lack of real-time tracking
No unified emergency transport platform
Manual coordination between hospitals and drivers

These delays can increase the risk during critical emergencies where every minute can impact patient survival.

The ResQ Solution

ResQ is a technology platform that connects patients, ambulance drivers, and healthcare facilities through a real-time digital network.

When a user requests an ambulance through the ResQ mobile application, the system instantly locates the nearest available ambulance and dispatches it to the patient’s location.

Using location technology and smart dispatch systems, ResQ helps reduce response time and improves coordination during emergencies.

Real-time ambulance discovery
GPS-based dispatch system
Live ambulance tracking
Direct communication between driver and patient
Quick and simple booking interface

ResQ is designed to make emergency transportation faster, simpler, and more reliable.

How ResQ Works

1️⃣ Request

A user opens the ResQ app and requests an ambulance from their current location.
2️⃣ Match

The platform identifies the nearest available ambulance from the network.
3️⃣ Dispatch

The ambulance driver accepts the request and starts the trip immediately.
4️⃣ Track

The user can track the ambulance in real time until it reaches the pickup location.

Ambulance Network Platform

ResQ operates as a digital platform that connects independent ambulance providers and fleet partners with patients who need emergency transportation.

Instead of owning ambulances, the platform builds a strong network of existing ambulance drivers and operators.

This model allows:

Faster expansion of ambulance availability
Increased earning opportunities for drivers
Improved emergency accessibility for patients

Drivers can choose when to go online and accept ride requests through the ResQ driver application.

Business Model

ResQ operates on a commission-based platform model.

Ambulance providers and fleet operators can join the network and receive emergency ride requests through the ResQ driver application.

For every completed trip, ResQ charges a 15% platform commission, while the remaining amount goes directly to the ambulance provider.

Drivers get more ride opportunities
Patients get faster emergency access
The platform scales efficiently
